\begin{cabstract}
合成孔径雷达（Synthetic Aperture Radar, SAR）自动目标识别在军事侦察和战场态势感知中具有重要应用价值，但受相干斑噪声、成像条件变化和平台资源约束影响，现有方法仍面临实测数据稀缺、跨域分布偏移显著和边缘部署受限等问题。本文围绕仿真到实测迁移场景下的跨域SAR目标识别展开研究，针对“仿真与少量实测数据联合训练”和“纯仿真数据训练”两类场景，分别研究域适应与域泛化方法，并完成国产AI芯片平台上的轻量化部署。主要工作如下：

（1）针对少量实测数据条件下跨域对齐困难的问题，提出基于像素级域对齐和类混淆损失的域适应SAR目标识别方法。该方法利用基于对比学习的非配对图像翻译实现少样本像素级对齐，并结合类混淆损失和一致性正则化优化实测域判别边界，从而降低模型对实测数据量的依赖。SAMPLE数据集实验表明，所提方法在实测样本极少时仍能保持较高识别性能。

（2）针对训练阶段实测数据不可见条件下模型泛化能力不足的问题，提出基于多层级数据增强和对抗学习的域泛化SAR目标识别方法。该方法联合像素级增强、特征级风格混淆和域对抗学习，提升仿真数据分布多样性并提取域不变特征，从而增强模型对未知实测域的泛化能力。SAMPLE和S2M-5数据集实验表明，所提方法具有较好的识别性能和跨域稳定性。

（3）面向国产AI芯片部署需求，提出跨域SAR目标识别模型轻量化方案。该方案采用ResNet50教师模型和MobileNetV2学生模型构建知识蒸馏框架，并结合基于AMCT的INT8后训练量化与Atlas 200I DK A2端侧部署流程，在显著降低模型规模、存储开销和推理时延的同时，较好保持了跨域识别性能。


\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) automatic target recognition is of great importance for military reconnaissance and battlefield situational awareness. However, practical applications are still challenged by speckle noise, variations in imaging conditions, scarce measured data, significant cross-domain shifts, and limited edge resources. This thesis studies cross-domain SAR target recognition for simulated-to-measured transfer. For the two settings of training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, and training with purely simulated data, domain adaptation and domain generalization methods are investigated, followed by lightweight deployment on a domestically developed AI chip platform. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) For the setting with limited measured data, a domain adaptation method based on pixel-level alignment and class confusion loss is proposed. A contrastive-learning-based unpaired image translation model is used for few-shot pixel-level alignment, and class confusion loss with consistency regularization is introduced to optimize the decision boundary on the measured domain, thereby reducing dependence on measured samples. Experiments on the SAMPLE dataset show that the proposed method maintains high recognition performance even when measured samples are scarce.

(2) For the setting where measured data are unavailable during training, a domain generalization method based on multi-level data augmentation and adversarial learning is proposed. By jointly using pixel-level augmentation, feature-level style confusion, and domain adversarial learning, the method enriches simulated data diversity and learns domain-invariant features, thus improving generalization to unseen measured domains. Experiments on the SAMPLE and S2M-5 datasets demonstrate favorable recognition performance and cross-domain stability.

(3) For domestic AI chip deployment, a lightweight scheme for cross-domain SAR target recognition models is developed. A knowledge distillation framework with a ResNet50 teacher and a MobileNetV2 student is combined with AMCT-based INT8 post-training quantization and deployment on the Huawei Atlas 200I DK A2. Results show that the scheme substantially reduces model size, storage cost, and inference latency while preserving cross-domain recognition performance.

\end{eabstract}
