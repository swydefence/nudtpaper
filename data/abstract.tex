\begin{cabstract}
合成孔径雷达（Synthetic Aperture Radar, SAR）自动目标识别在军事侦察和战场态势感知中具有重要应用价值。受SAR成像机理影响，图像中普遍存在相干斑噪声，且目标在不同传感器参数、观测角度和成像条件下易产生显著的特征差异，使得基于单一数据分布训练的识别模型难以直接适应复杂实测场景。与此同时，实测样本获取与标注成本较高，进一步加剧了面向仿真-实测迁移的跨域SAR目标识别建模难度。本文围绕仿真数据向实测场景迁移过程中的跨域SAR目标识别问题展开研究，针对“面向仿真与少量实测数据训练”和“面向纯仿真数据训练”两类典型场景，分别研究域适应与域泛化方法，并进一步开展轻量化部署验证，以提升模型在不同实测条件下的识别性能与应用可行性。主要工作如下：

（1）针对面向仿真与少量实测数据训练场景下实测样本稀缺、跨域对齐困难的问题，提出基于像素级域对齐和类混淆损失的域适应SAR目标识别方法。该方法通过基于对比学习的非配对图像翻译实现少样本条件下的域间外观对齐，并结合类混淆损失与一致性正则化优化实测域判别边界，从而在降低对实测数据分布统计依赖的同时提升跨域识别性能。SAMPLE数据集实验结果表明，所提方法在实测样本极少条件下仍具有较高识别精度。

（2）针对面向纯仿真数据训练场景下训练阶段实测域完全不可见、模型易过拟合仿真域的问题，提出基于多层级数据增强和对抗学习的域泛化SAR目标识别方法。该方法构建由像素级增强、特征级风格混淆和域对抗学习组成的协同框架，从输入分布扩展、深层风格扰动和域不变特征约束三个层面抑制域偏移，从而提升模型对未知实测域的泛化能力。SAMPLE和S2M-5数据集实验结果表明，所提方法具有较好的识别性能和跨域稳定性。

（3）针对跨域SAR目标识别模型在国产端侧平台部署中的精度、复杂度与实时性平衡问题，提出轻量化部署方案。该方案通过知识蒸馏与INT8后训练量化实现模型压缩，并在华为Atlas 200I DK A2平台完成部署验证，在显著降低模型规模和推理时延的同时较好保持了跨域识别性能，为相关方法的工程应用提供了支撑。


\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) automatic target recognition is of great importance for military reconnaissance and battlefield situational awareness. Due to the SAR imaging mechanism, images are corrupted by speckle noise, and target characteristics vary significantly with sensor parameters, viewing angles, and imaging conditions, making models trained on a single data distribution difficult to generalize to complex measured scenarios. Meanwhile, measured SAR samples are costly to acquire and annotate, which further increases the difficulty of cross-domain SAR target recognition for simulated-to-measured transfer. This thesis focuses on cross-domain SAR target recognition in the transfer from simulated data to measured scenarios. For the two typical settings of training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, and training with purely simulated data, domain adaptation and domain generalization methods are investigated, followed by lightweight deployment validation to improve both recognition performance under different measured conditions and application feasibility. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) For the setting of training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, a domain adaptation method based on pixel-level alignment and class confusion loss is proposed to address the problems of scarce measured samples and difficult cross-domain alignment. The method performs cross-domain appearance alignment under few-shot conditions via contrastive-learning-based unpaired image translation, and further optimizes the decision boundary on the measured domain through class confusion loss and consistency regularization. In this way, it improves cross-domain recognition performance while reducing dependence on measured data statistics. Experiments on the SAMPLE dataset show that the proposed method maintains high recognition accuracy even when measured samples are extremely limited.

(2) For the setting of training with purely simulated data, a domain generalization method based on multi-level data augmentation and adversarial learning is proposed to address the problem that the measured domain is entirely unavailable during training and the model tends to overfit the simulated domain. The method constructs a collaborative framework consisting of pixel-level augmentation, feature-level style confusion, and domain adversarial learning, which suppresses domain shift from the three aspects of input distribution expansion, deep style perturbation, and domain-invariant feature constraints. As a result, it improves generalization to unseen measured domains. Experiments on the SAMPLE and S2M-5 datasets demonstrate favorable recognition performance and cross-domain stability.

(3) To balance accuracy, complexity, and real-time performance for deployment of cross-domain SAR target recognition models on domestic edge platforms, a lightweight deployment scheme is developed. The scheme combines knowledge distillation with INT8 post-training quantization and is validated on the Huawei Atlas 200I DK A2 platform. Results show that it significantly reduces model size and inference latency while preserving cross-domain recognition performance, providing support for engineering application of the proposed methods.

\end{eabstract}
