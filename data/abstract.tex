\begin{cabstract}
合成孔径雷达（Synthetic Aperture Radar, SAR）自动目标识别在军事侦察和战场态势感知中具有重要应用价值。受SAR成像机理影响，图像中普遍存在相干斑噪声，且目标在不同传感器参数、观测角度和成像条件下易产生显著的特征差异，使得基于单一数据分布训练的识别模型难以直接适应复杂实测场景。与此同时，实测样本获取与标注成本较高，进一步加剧了跨域SAR目标识别的建模难度。本文围绕仿真到实测迁移场景下的跨域SAR目标识别展开研究，针对“仿真与少量实测数据联合训练”和“纯仿真数据训练”两类典型场景，分别研究域适应与域泛化方法，并完成国产AI芯片平台上的轻量化部署，以提升模型在不同实测可见条件下的识别性能与工程应用能力。主要工作如下：

（1）针对少量实测数据条件下跨域对齐困难、现有无监督域适应方法依赖较多实测样本的问题，提出基于像素级域对齐和类混淆损失的域适应SAR目标识别方法。该方法在像素级引入基于对比学习的非配对图像翻译模型，实现少样本条件下的高质量域间外观对齐；在特征级结合类混淆损失和一致性正则化，直接优化实测域判别边界，从而提升域对齐与类别判别能力，降低对实测数据分布统计的依赖。SAMPLE数据集实验表明，所提方法在实测样本极少时仍能保持较高识别性能。

（2）针对训练阶段实测数据完全不可见条件下模型易过拟合仿真域、对未知实测域泛化不足的问题，提出基于多层级数据增强和对抗学习的域泛化SAR目标识别方法。该方法构建由像素级增强、特征级风格混淆和域对抗学习组成的协同框架，其中像素级增强依据SAR图像特性扩展仿真数据分布，特征级风格混淆模拟多样化域风格，域对抗学习抑制域特异性信息，从而提升模型对未知实测域的鲁棒泛化能力。SAMPLE和S2M-5数据集实验表明，所提方法具有较好的识别性能和跨域稳定性。

（3）面向国产AI芯片部署需求，提出跨域SAR目标识别模型轻量化方案。该方案采用ResNet50教师模型和MobileNetV2学生模型构建知识蒸馏框架，并结合基于AMCT的INT8后训练量化与Atlas 200I DK A2端侧部署流程，在显著降低模型规模、存储开销和推理时延的同时较好保持了跨域识别性能，为相关模型的国产化端侧应用提供了技术支撑。


\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) automatic target recognition is of great importance for military reconnaissance and battlefield situational awareness. Due to the SAR imaging mechanism, images are corrupted by speckle noise, and target characteristics vary significantly with sensor parameters, viewing angles, and imaging conditions, making models trained on a single data distribution difficult to generalize to complex measured scenarios. Meanwhile, measured SAR samples are costly to acquire and annotate, which further increases the difficulty of cross-domain SAR target recognition. This thesis studies cross-domain SAR target recognition for simulated-to-measured transfer. For the two typical settings of training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, and training with purely simulated data, domain adaptation and domain generalization methods are investigated, followed by lightweight deployment on a domestically developed AI chip platform to improve both recognition performance and engineering applicability. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) For the setting with limited measured data, a domain adaptation method based on pixel-level alignment and class confusion loss is proposed. A contrastive-learning-based unpaired image translation model is introduced to achieve high-quality alignment under few-shot conditions, while class confusion loss and consistency regularization are used to optimize the decision boundary on the measured domain. In this way, the method improves both cross-domain alignment and category discrimination while reducing dependence on measured data statistics. Experiments on the SAMPLE dataset show that the proposed method maintains high recognition performance even when measured samples are scarce.

(2) For the setting where measured data are unavailable during training, a domain generalization method based on multi-level data augmentation and adversarial learning is proposed. The method integrates pixel-level augmentation, feature-level style confusion, and domain adversarial learning to enrich the diversity of simulated data and suppress domain-specific information, thereby improving robustness and generalization to unseen measured domains. Experiments on the SAMPLE and S2M-5 datasets demonstrate favorable recognition performance and cross-domain stability.

(3) For domestic AI chip deployment, a lightweight scheme for cross-domain SAR target recognition models is developed. A knowledge distillation framework with a ResNet50 teacher and a MobileNetV2 student is combined with AMCT-based INT8 post-training quantization and deployment on the Huawei Atlas 200I DK A2. Results show that the scheme substantially reduces model size, storage cost, and inference latency while preserving cross-domain recognition performance.

\end{eabstract}
