\begin{cabstract}
实测合成孔径雷达（Synthetic Aperture Radar，SAR）图像获取成本高、标注难度大，在复杂任务场景下往往难以获得充足的高质量标注样本，因此利用仿真SAR图像训练目标识别模型已成为一种重要替代方案。然而，仿真SAR图像与实测SAR图像在背景噪声、目标纹理和统计分布等方面均存在显著差异，即仿真域与实测域之间存在固有域偏移，导致基于仿真数据训练的模型难以直接应用于实测场景。为此，本文以仿真数据域为源域、实测数据域为目标域，围绕面向仿真-实测迁移的跨域SAR目标识别方法开展研究。重点针对“仿真与少量无标注实测数据协同训练”和“纯仿真数据训练”两类典型场景分别开展创新设计与技术验证，并进一步实现跨域识别模型在国产 AI 芯片上的轻量化部署。主要工作如下：

（1）针对仿真与少量无标注实测数据协同训练场景下目标域信息不足、跨域迁移困难的问题，提出了一种基于像素级域对齐和类混淆损失的无监督域适应SAR目标识别方法。在像素级上通过图像块对比学习降低风格迁移模型对目标域样本的依赖，使仿真图像在少量实测样本条件下仍能稳定迁移到实测风格；在特征级上结合类混淆损失与一致性约束，直接优化少量无标注实测样本的判别边界，减少对目标域整体分布估计的依赖。实验结果表明，所提方法有效缓解了现有域适应方法在实测样本不足条件下跨域迁移失效、模型识别性能不足的问题。

（2）针对纯仿真数据训练场景下目标域完全不可见、模型易过拟合于源域的问题，提出了一种基于多层级数据增强和对抗学习的域泛化SAR目标识别方法。在像素级上结合高斯噪声注入、高频扰动和分贝化变换，从多维度扩展仿真数据分布；在特征级上引入风格混淆模块对实例级特征统计量进行随机混合，模拟潜在域风格变化并抑制模型对特定源域特征的过拟合，进一步通过域对抗学习约束模型提取域不变判别特征。实验结果表明，所提方法显著提升了基于纯仿真数据训练的SAR目标识别模型面向未见实测数据的泛化能力和识别性能。

（3）针对跨域SAR目标识别模型在国产边缘平台部署过程中参数规模较大、推理开销较高的问题，提出了一种面向华为昇腾芯片的轻量化部署方案。该方案结合知识蒸馏与INT8后训练量化，实现了跨域识别模型的压缩与边缘设备部署验证。实验结果表明，该方案在较好保持跨域识别性能的同时，能够有效降低模型复杂度与推理开销，为国产边缘设备上的实际部署应用提供了支撑。

\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Measured synthetic aperture radar (SAR) images are expensive to acquire and difficult to annotate, and in complex task scenarios it is often hard to obtain sufficient high-quality labeled samples. Therefore, training target recognition models with simulated SAR images has become an important alternative. However, simulated and measured SAR images differ significantly in background noise, target texture, and statistical distribution, revealing an inherent domain shift between the simulated and measured domains. As a result, models trained on simulated data are difficult to apply directly to measured scenarios. To address this issue, this thesis takes the simulated data domain as the source domain and the measured data domain as the target domain, and studies cross-domain SAR target recognition methods for simulated-to-measured transfer. Focusing on two typical scenarios, namely collaborative training with simulated data and a small amount of unlabeled measured data, and training with purely simulated data, this thesis carries out innovative method design and technical validation, and further realizes the lightweight deployment of cross-domain recognition models on domestic AI chips. The main work is summarized as follows:

(1) To address the insufficient target-domain information and difficult cross-domain transfer in the collaborative training scenario with simulated data and a small amount of unlabeled measured data, this thesis proposes an unsupervised domain adaptation method for SAR target recognition based on pixel-level domain alignment and class confusion loss. At the pixel level, patch-based contrastive learning is used to reduce the dependence of the style transfer model on target-domain samples, so that simulated images can still be stably translated into the measured style under limited unlabeled measured samples. At the feature level, class confusion loss and consistency constraints are combined to directly optimize the target-domain decision boundary under limited unlabeled measured samples, thereby reducing reliance on estimating the overall target-domain distribution. Experimental results show that the proposed method effectively alleviates the failure of existing unsupervised domain adaptation methods in cross-domain transfer and their insufficient recognition performance when measured samples are scarce.

(2) To address the problems that the target domain is completely inaccessible and the model tends to overfit the source domain in the purely simulated-data training scenario, this thesis proposes a domain generalization method for SAR target recognition based on multi-level data augmentation and adversarial learning. At the pixel level, Gaussian noise injection, high-frequency perturbation, and decibel transformation are combined to expand the distribution of simulated data from multiple dimensions. At the feature level, a style confusion module is introduced to randomly mix instance-level feature statistics, thereby simulating potential domain-style variations and suppressing the model's overfitting to features of a specific source domain. Domain adversarial learning is further employed to constrain the model to extract domain-invariant discriminative features. Experimental results show that the proposed method significantly improves the generalization capability and recognition performance of SAR target recognition models trained with purely simulated data on unseen measured data.

(3) To address the problems of large parameter scale and high inference overhead during the deployment of cross-domain SAR target recognition models on domestic edge platforms, this thesis proposes a lightweight deployment scheme for Huawei Ascend chips. By combining knowledge distillation with INT8 post-training quantization, the scheme realizes the compression of cross-domain recognition models and validates their deployment on edge devices. Experimental results show that the scheme effectively reduces model complexity and inference overhead while preserving cross-domain recognition performance, thereby supporting practical deployment on domestic edge devices.
