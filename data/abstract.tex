\begin{cabstract}
由于实测合成孔径雷达（Synthetic Aperture Radar，SAR）图像获取成本高、周期长，且在复杂任务场景下往往难以获得充足的高质量样本，利用仿真SAR图像训练目标识别模型已成为一种重要替代方案。随着计算机辅助设计与电磁仿真技术的发展，大规模生成具有准确标签的仿真SAR图像已具备可行性。然而，仿真SAR图像与实测SAR图像在噪声背景、纹理细节和统计分布等方面均不可避免地存在显著差异，即仿真域与实测域之间存在域偏移，导致基于仿真数据训练的模型难以直接适应实测场景。为此，本文围绕面向仿真-实测迁移的跨域SAR目标识别方法开展研究，针对面向仿真与少量实测数据协同训练和面向纯仿真数据训练两类场景分别开展方法设计与验证，并进一步实现了基于国产AI芯片的部署。主要工作如下：

（1）针对面向仿真与少量实测数据训练场景下目标域信息不足、域间对齐困难的问题，提出了一种基于像素级域对齐和类混淆损失的域适应SAR目标识别方法。该方法利用基于对比学习的非配对图像翻译模型实现仿真图像向实测风格的像素级迁移，并结合类混淆损失与一致性约束优化少量实测样本条件下的目标域判别边界。实验结果表明，所提方法仅需少量实测样本即可有效提升模型的跨域识别性能与稳定性。该方法有效缓解了少量实测样本参与训练时仿真域与实测域难以稳定对齐、模型跨域识别能力不足的问题。

（2）针对面向纯仿真数据训练场景下训练阶段目标域完全不可见、模型易过拟合仿真域的问题，提出了一种基于多层级数据增强和对抗学习的域泛化SAR目标识别方法。该方法通过像素级数据增强扩展仿真数据分布，利用特征级风格混淆模拟多样化域风格，并结合域对抗学习约束模型提取域不变特征。实验结果表明，所提方法能够有效提高模型在未知实测场景中的泛化能力与跨域稳定性。该方法有效缓解了训练阶段无法获取实测数据时模型对单一仿真分布依赖过强、难以泛化到未知实测场景的问题。

（3）针对跨域SAR目标识别模型在国产边缘平台部署过程中参数规模较大、推理开销较高的问题，提出了一种面向国产AI芯片平台的轻量化部署方案。该方案结合知识蒸馏与INT8后训练量化，实现了跨域识别模型的压缩与板端部署验证。实验结果表明，该方案在较好保持跨域识别性能的同时，能够有效降低模型复杂度与推理开销。该方案有效缓解了跨域SAR目标识别模型在国产边缘设备上部署成本较高、实时性受限的问题，为实际部署应用提供了支撑。
\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Because measured Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) images are costly and time-consuming to acquire, and sufficient high-quality samples are often unavailable in complex operational scenarios, using simulated SAR images to train target recognition models has become an important alternative. With the development of computer-aided design and electromagnetic simulation techniques, large-scale generation of simulated SAR images with accurate labels has become feasible. However, simulated and measured SAR images inevitably exhibit significant discrepancies in background noise, texture details, and statistical distributions, resulting in domain shift between the simulated and measured domains and making it difficult for models trained on simulated data to generalize directly to measured scenarios. To address this issue, this thesis investigates cross-domain SAR target recognition methods for simulated-to-measured transfer. Method design and validation are conducted for two scenarios, namely collaborative training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, and training with purely simulated data; in addition, deployment on a domestic AI chip platform is further implemented. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) For the scenario of training with simulated data and a small amount of measured data, a domain adaptation method for SAR target recognition based on pixel-level domain alignment and class confusion loss is proposed to address the problems of insufficient target-domain information and difficult cross-domain alignment. The method performs pixel-level translation of simulated images toward the measured style via a contrastive-learning-based unpaired image translation model, and further optimizes the decision boundary in the target domain under limited measured samples through class confusion loss and consistency regularization. Experimental results show that the proposed method can effectively improve cross-domain recognition performance and stability with only a small amount of measured data. This method effectively alleviates the problem that, when only a few measured samples are involved in training, the simulated and measured domains are difficult to align stably and the model exhibits insufficient cross-domain recognition capability.

(2) For the scenario of training with purely simulated data, a domain generalization method for SAR target recognition based on multi-level data augmentation and adversarial learning is proposed to address the problem that the target domain is completely unavailable during training and the model tends to overfit the simulated domain. The method expands the distribution of simulated data through pixel-level data augmentation, simulates diverse domain styles through feature-level style confusion, and constrains the model to extract domain-invariant features through domain adversarial learning. Experimental results show that the proposed method can effectively improve generalization ability and cross-domain stability in unseen measured scenarios. This method effectively alleviates the problem that, when measured data are unavailable during training, the model depends excessively on a single simulated distribution and is therefore difficult to generalize to unseen measured scenarios.

(3) To address the problems of large model size and high inference overhead during the deployment of cross-domain SAR target recognition models on domestic edge platforms, a lightweight deployment scheme for a domestic AI chip platform is proposed. The scheme combines knowledge distillation with INT8 post-training quantization to achieve model compression and on-board deployment validation for cross-domain recognition models. Experimental results show that the scheme can effectively reduce model complexity and inference overhead while largely preserving cross-domain recognition performance. This scheme effectively alleviates the problems of high deployment cost and limited real-time performance of cross-domain SAR target recognition models on domestic edge devices, thereby providing support for practical deployment.

\end{eabstract}
